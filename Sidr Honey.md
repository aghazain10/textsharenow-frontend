## **Our Story** 

Our story began with a simple childhood experience that stayed with me. I struggled with a persistent cough, and I remember how pure Sidr honey became something I personally relied on when nothing else seemed to help. That experience created a lasting appreciation for the natural richness of Sidr honey. 

Years later, Sidr honey remains part of my daily routine. I enjoy it regularly, often alongside shilajit, because it is something I genuinely value and have trusted for years. 

But our story is not only about honey. It is also about bees. Their discipline, teamwork, resilience, and remarkable precision have always inspired me. During the short Sidr flowering season, they work with incredible focus, gathering nectar from blooming Sidr flowers and transforming it into something truly special. 

That natural process taught me the importance of patience, purpose, and respect for nature. These values are at the heart of **SDR Honey** . 

Today, our goal is simple: to share authentic Sidr honey from the wild forests of Karak with people around the world, while honoring the bees, the land, and the natural process behind every jar.
